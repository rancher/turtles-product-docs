== What does Certified Providers mean?

Certified Providers are CAPI Providers validated to work with the CAPI extension for Rancher. There are two groups:

* Providers certified by SUSE are **actively tested** by the Turtles team's CI workflows to validate they work properly with the CAPI extension for Rancher. However, since most CAPI Providers are upstream projects maintained by the Kubernetes open community, **we cannot guarantee** that no issues are introduced as new provider versions are released.
* Providers certified by partners are maintained by SUSE partners. Each partner runs the xref:../operator/certificationsuite.adoc[Turtles certification test suite] against a declared set of Rancher, Turtles and provider versions, and submits the results to SUSE.

CAPG::
